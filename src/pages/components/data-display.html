turn your boat purchase into a wonderful vacation in the ozarks!

With the Cast Off signature boating experience, boat customers nationwide can choose to pick up their new boat right where it’s built in the Ozarks and save on freight charges that vary by location. It’s more than a pickup. It’s a red carpet experience that turns delivery into a family getaway, complete with exclusive savings, behind-the-scenes plant tours, special experience and resort offers, and the opportunity to take your new boat out on the water at nearby Table Rock Lake!

How it works

Order your new boat in-store or by phone

Work with one of our online sales team members by calling 417-815-5409 or visiting your local store’s Boating Center to speak with a sales team member! Let them know that you want to take delivery the Cast Off way during your purchase process.

Choose your pickup experience options

We’ll connect you with a friendly VIP Boat Concierge who will help you every step of the way! They’ll send you options to build your perfect visit—from lodging options with special discounts to once-in-a-lifetime experiences and VIP boat plant tours.

Pick up your boat with a special VIP experience at the Grandaddy of all outdoors stores!

When your boat is ready for pickup, you’ll make the trip to the Springfield, Missouri Bass Pro Shops. We’ll make it special for you and the whole family!
