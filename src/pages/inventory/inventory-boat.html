2019 TRACKER Pro Team 190 TXW

Springfield, MO

America's Favorite Boat

The TRACKER PRO TEAM 175 TXW® Tournament Edition is fully equipped with the tools you need to tackle the tournament trail. With a big bow deck laid out just like the pros demand, plus dual color graphs, a 24V trolling motor and a 21-gallon livewell, it’s designed to help you find fish, catch them and get you back to the weigh-in fast.

For your peace of mind, the unparalleled quality of this all-welded boat is backed by the TRACKER® PROMISE—the best factory warranty in aluminum boats, with 5-year bow-to-stern coverage and a lifetime deck and hull structure warrant
